# FinServe Bank – Data Governance Implementation Roadmap & Value Realisation

## Purpose

This roadmap defines a phased approach for implementing and embedding Data Governance across FinServe Bank.

The approach connects governance priorities, Data Quality improvement, metadata management, regulatory compliance, AI Governance, technology enablement and organisational adoption with measurable business outcomes.

## Implementation Approach

The programme progresses through four stages:

### Stage 1 – Assess & Design

Key activities:

- Current-state Data Governance assessment
- Regulatory and governance gap analysis
- Stakeholder identification and engagement
- Data Governance Charter
- Critical Data Inventory
- Governance quick wins
- Initial implementation roadmap

Primary objective:

**Establish the governance baseline and prioritise the most significant data and regulatory risks.**

### Stage 2 – Govern & Control

Key activities:

- Federated Data Governance operating model
- Data Governance Council
- Data Owner and Data Steward responsibilities
- RACI matrix
- Governance policies and standards
- Data Quality Framework
- Metadata Management Framework
- Data classification
- Privacy and PII governance
- Data retention and lifecycle controls
- AI Governance Framework

Primary objective:

**Establish clear governance accountability, standards, policies and controls.**

### Stage 3 – Operationalise & Monitor

Key activities:

- Data profiling
- Data Quality rules
- Root-cause analysis
- Data Quality monitoring
- Issue management and remediation
- Metadata management
- Data catalogue design
- Data lineage documentation
- PII inventory
- AI Model Cards
- AI risk assessment
- Bias and fairness assessment
- Governance monitoring

Primary objective:

**Move governance from documented frameworks into repeatable operational processes.**

### Stage 4 – Integrate & Realise Value

Key activities:

- Governance KPI monitoring
- Executive scorecards
- Dashboard design
- Integration planning
- Change management
- Governance communication
- Training and adoption
- Business-value assessment
- Executive reporting
- Strategic governance planning

Primary objective:

**Embed governance into business operations and demonstrate measurable organisational value.**

## 12-Month Implementation Priorities

The initial programme roadmap focuses on six connected workstreams:

| Workstream | Priority Activities | Intended Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Governance Operating Model | Establish Council, ownership, stewardship and decision rights | Clear accountability |
| Data Quality | Profiling, rules, monitoring, remediation and root-cause analysis | Improved trusted data |
| Metadata & Catalogue | Definitions, metadata standards, catalogue and CDE governance | Improved data discovery and understanding |
| Privacy & Lifecycle | Classification, PII protection, access, retention and lifecycle controls | Reduced regulatory and privacy risk |
| AI Governance | Model inventory, risk classification, impact assessment and monitoring | Responsible and controlled AI use |
| Adoption & Monitoring | Training, communications, KPIs, dashboards and governance reviews | Sustainable governance adoption |

## Quick Wins

Early governance actions were prioritised to demonstrate value while the wider programme was being established.

Priority areas included:

- Clarifying Data Owner and Data Steward responsibilities
- Addressing significant customer Data Quality issues
- Establishing governance escalation
- Identifying Critical Data Elements
- Improving sensitive-data classification and protection
- Introducing governance monitoring
- Establishing AI governance oversight

Quick wins support early risk reduction while building momentum for longer-term governance implementation.

## Governance Performance

Governance performance is measured across:

- Data Quality
- Governance accountability
- Metadata
- Compliance
- AI Governance
- Operations
- Adoption
- Risk

The executive framework contains 16 governance KPIs with assigned ownership, thresholds and escalation requirements.

Performance should be monitored operationally and reviewed with leadership on a regular basis.

## Change Management & Adoption

Successful governance requires organisational adoption in addition to policies and technology.

The change approach includes:

- Stakeholder engagement
- Governance communications
- Role-specific guidance
- Data Owner and Data Steward engagement
- Governance training
- Leadership sponsorship
- Regular governance forums
- KPI reporting
- Feedback and continuous improvement

A training-completion target of **≥90%** was incorporated into the governance performance framework.

## Value Realisation

The governance programme is designed to generate value through:

- Reduced manual data correction and reconciliation
- Reduced reporting errors and rework
- Improved operational efficiency
- Improved decision-making
- Stronger regulatory compliance
- Reduced data risk
- Reduced AI risk
- Increased accountability
- Improved organisational trust in data

No unsupported financial ROI values are assumed. Value is assessed through governance KPIs, operational improvements, risk reduction and measurable changes in Data Quality and governance performance.

## Three-Year Data Governance Strategy

### Year 1 – Foundation

**Focus:** Governance setup and Data Quality remediation

Key priorities:

- Establish governance structures
- Assign ownership and stewardship
- Implement priority policies and controls
- Address significant Data Quality issues
- Strengthen regulatory governance

**Strategic impact:** Risk reduction

### Year 2 – Expansion

**Focus:** Metadata, catalogue and lineage

Key priorities:

- Expand metadata management
- Increase catalogue coverage
- Improve business glossary adoption
- Document critical data lineage
- Strengthen CDE governance
- Improve enterprise data visibility

**Strategic impact:** Improved data visibility

### Year 3 – Optimisation

**Focus:** AI Governance and automation

Key priorities:

- Mature AI Governance
- Expand automated monitoring
- Strengthen model governance
- Improve governance analytics
- Increase automation of governance controls
- Support advanced analytics responsibly

**Strategic impact:** Advanced analytics and sustainable governance
