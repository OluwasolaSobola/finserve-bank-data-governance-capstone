# FinServe Bank – Data Governance Charter

## Purpose

The Data Governance Charter establishes the mandate, objectives, governance structure, decision rights and accountability required to govern FinServe Bank's data as an enterprise asset.

The Charter supports the MBanking 2025 digital transformation, regulatory compliance, Data Quality improvement and planned merger integration.

## Governance Vision

To establish trusted, well-managed and appropriately governed data that supports regulatory compliance, operational effectiveness, customer trust and informed decision-making.

## Governance Objectives

The Data Governance programme aims to:

- Improve Data Quality and reliability
- Strengthen regulatory compliance
- Establish clear data ownership and accountability
- Protect sensitive and critical information
- Establish consistent governance policies and standards
- Improve metadata management and data understanding
- Strengthen AI Governance and Responsible AI
- Support MBanking 2025
- Enable effective merger data integration
- Establish measurable governance performance

## Scope

The governance programme covers:

- Customer data
- Product data
- Transaction data
- Risk data
- Finance data
- Critical Data Elements
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Metadata
- Data Quality
- Data access and sharing
- Data retention and lifecycle
- AI models and AI-related data

## Governance Principles

1. Data is treated as a strategic enterprise asset.
2. Data ownership and accountability must be clearly defined.
3. Data Quality must be measurable and actively managed.
4. Sensitive data must receive protection appropriate to its risk.
5. Governance requirements should be embedded into business processes.
6. Metadata and business definitions should be consistent and accessible.
7. Data issues should have clear ownership, escalation and resolution processes.
8. AI should be governed according to risk and Responsible AI principles.
9. Governance performance should be measurable and transparent.
10. Governance should support business value as well as regulatory compliance.

## Governance Structure

FinServe Bank uses a federated Data Governance model combining central governance oversight with domain-level accountability.

### Data Governance Council

Responsible for:

- Governance oversight
- Policy approval
- Governance performance monitoring
- Resolution of escalated governance issues
- Strategic governance decisions

### Data Owners

Responsible for:

- Accountability for domain data
- Business definitions
- Data Quality
- Access decisions
- Resolution of significant data issues

### Data Stewards

Responsible for:

- Day-to-day Data Quality monitoring
- Application of governance standards
- Metadata maintenance
- Identification and management of data issues
- Escalation where required

### IT / Data Team

Responsible for supporting the technical implementation of governance controls, systems and data integration requirements.

### Compliance

Provides regulatory oversight and supports monitoring of compliance requirements.

## Governance Forums & Cadence

- **Data Governance Council:** Monthly
- **Domain Stewardship Forums:** Bi-weekly
- **Data Quality Reviews:** Monthly

## Decision & Escalation Model

Operational governance matters are managed at the appropriate stewardship or ownership level.

Significant unresolved issues follow the escalation path:

**Data Steward → Data Owner → Data Governance Council**

## Governance Performance

Governance effectiveness is monitored through KPIs covering:

- Data Quality
- Data ownership
- Policy compliance
- Metadata and catalogue coverage
- Data lineage
- KYC completion
- AI model monitoring
- Issue resolution
- Training and adoption
- Data incidents
