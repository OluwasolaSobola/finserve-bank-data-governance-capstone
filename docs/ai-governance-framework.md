# FinServe Bank – AI Governance & Responsible AI Framework

## Purpose

The AI Governance Framework establishes a risk-based approach for governing AI models across FinServe Bank.

The framework is designed to strengthen accountability, transparency, fairness, privacy, reliability and appropriate oversight throughout the AI model lifecycle.

## Responsible AI Principles

The framework incorporates five core Responsible AI principles:

- Fairness
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Privacy
- Reliability

These principles guide model development, assessment, approval, monitoring and governance decisions.

## AI Risk Classification

A four-tier model was established:

### Tier 1 – Low Risk

Models with limited business, customer or regulatory impact.

Governance requirements focus on appropriate documentation and monitoring.

### Tier 2 – Moderate Risk

Models with greater influence on business processes or customer interactions.

Additional governance review, monitoring and documentation are required.

### Tier 3 – High Risk

Models with significant operational, customer, financial or compliance implications.

Enhanced validation, risk assessment, monitoring and governance oversight are required.

### Tier 4 – Critical Risk

Models associated with significant regulatory, financial, customer or decision-making consequences.

These models require the strongest governance controls, documentation, validation, approval, monitoring and oversight.

## AI Governance Controls

Governance requirements include:

- Defined model purpose and intended use
- Assigned model ownership and accountability
- AI risk classification
- Documented data sources
- Data Quality assessment
- Model validation and testing
- Bias and fairness assessment
- Privacy assessment
- Model documentation
- Model Cards
- AI Impact Assessment
- Performance monitoring
- Periodic governance review
- Escalation of significant model risks

Governance requirements increase according to the model's assigned risk tier.

## AI Impact Assessment

An AI Impact Assessment approach was developed covering:

1. Model purpose and intended use
2. Data sources and Data Quality
3. Data sensitivity and PII
4. Business risk
5. Regulatory risk
6. Operational risk
7. Fairness and potential bias
8. Explainability and transparency
9. Privacy impact
10. Ownership and accountability
11. Affected stakeholders
12. Monitoring and ongoing validation

The assessment supports risk-based governance decisions before and during model use.

## Model Cards

Model Cards were developed for higher-risk AI use cases to provide structured documentation of:

- Model name and purpose
- Intended use
- Inputs and outputs
- Data sources
- Model owner
- Risk classification
- Key limitations
- Data Quality considerations
- Fairness and bias considerations
- Monitoring requirements
- Governance controls

Model Cards improve transparency, accountability and traceability across the AI lifecycle.

## Bias & Fairness Assessment

Bias assessment was incorporated into the AI Governance approach to identify potential differences in model outcomes across relevant groups.

The governance process considers:

- Potential sources of bias
- Data representativeness
- Differences in model outcomes
- Fairness considerations
- Appropriate mitigation actions
- Ongoing monitoring

Material fairness concerns should be escalated for governance review.

## AI Governance Lifecycle

The governance lifecycle follows:

**Inventory → Risk Classification → Impact Assessment → Validation → Approval → Monitoring → Review → Remediation**

Higher-risk models receive stronger governance oversight throughout this lifecycle.

## Roles & Accountability

### Data Governance Council

Provides oversight for significant AI governance risks and escalated decisions.

### Governance Lead

Coordinates the AI Governance framework with the wider enterprise Data Governance programme.

### AI / Model Owner

Accountable for the model's purpose, appropriate use, documentation and performance.

### Data Owners & Data Stewards

Support governance of the data used by AI models, including Data Quality, classification and appropriate use.

### Compliance

Supports regulatory, privacy and compliance assessment of higher-risk AI use cases.

## Monitoring

AI governance monitoring includes:

- Model performance
- Data Quality
- Bias and fairness indicators
- Compliance with governance requirements
- Completion of required model reviews
- Emerging model risks
- Remediation actions

The executive governance framework established a target of **100% monitoring coverage for governed AI models**.

## Governance Escalation

Significant AI risks, control failures or unresolved issues are escalated through the appropriate governance structure for review and decision-making.

Critical models require enhanced oversight due to their potential regulatory, customer and business impact.
